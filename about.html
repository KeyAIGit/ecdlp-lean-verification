Why we are building this

Research should be possible to inspect and continue.

We are interested in the gap between a plausible argument and a result another person can check. Our work combines formal proofs, bounded experiments, and records of the assumptions and unsuccessful approaches that shaped a decision.

KeyAI Research is the research program. Research OS is the verification workspace being developed through that work. The current system is a public reference deployment, with external usability and demand still to be established.

For sensitive findings, first request a private channel without including the finding itself.
