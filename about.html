Purpose of the published work

Research should be possible to inspect and continue.

We are interested in the gap between a plausible argument and a result another person can check. Our work combines formal proofs, bounded experiments, and records of the assumptions and unsuccessful approaches that shaped a decision.

Research OS is the reference verification workspace produced through that work. The initiative is inactive; external usability and demand were not established. No wallet or private-key recovery services are offered.

Contact and collaboration

A public place to start.

For a research question, a reproducibility issue, or an introduction, email ceo@keyai.org or contact the project on GitHub. Share a short, non-sensitive description and the relevant public source.

For sensitive findings, email a non-sensitive summary first so the maintainer can agree on a suitable reporting channel.
